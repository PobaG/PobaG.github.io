About

This page is all about me.

My name is Giovanni Palazzolo, and I am a student at the University of Boulder studying Computer Science and Business Operaions management.

I enjoy coding, eating vegetables, and fantasizing about situations where I have to defend myself and those I love.

Feel free to check out my GitHub.

My main goals in life are to live, laugh, and love.

I want to do this through creative mediums, such as storytelling, video creation, and business.

Mission

My mission is to combine technology, business, and creativity to build projects that are both useful and expressive, while creating a life centered on growth, storytelling, and meaningful work.

My biggest enemies in life are the upper management of the world. I'm a believer that workers should own the means of production, and that our society is moving closer and closer to a reality that allows us to express ourselves freely.

Not just freely in terms of our rights, but freely in terms of our finances. I refute to be a wage slave and stand for a fight against these corporate shackles.
